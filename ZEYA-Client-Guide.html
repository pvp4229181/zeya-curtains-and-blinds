ZEYA Curtains & Blinds

Website overview and admin guide

1. What you are getting

A premium, fast, mobile-friendly website for ZEYA, built on WordPress and hosted on your Hostinger domain. It includes:

  • Home, About Us, Our Process, FAQs, Contact, Terms and Review pages
  • 4 collection pages: Curtains, Blinds, Motorized, Curtain Accessories
  • 38 individual product pages, each with an enquiry link
  • Contact strip, WhatsApp and phone buttons, and a contact form
  • Layouts for phones, tablets and desktops

3. Going live on Hostinger

These steps are carried out once by the developer:

  1. Connect your domain to a Hostinger WordPress hosting plan.
  2. Install WordPress and create your admin login.
  3. Turn on the free SSL certificate so the site loads securely.
  4. Upload and activate the ZEYA theme.
  5. Create the site pages, set the homepage and menus, and enter your contact details.
  6. Test on phone and desktop, then launch.
We need from you: the domain name, Hostinger access (or an invite for the developer), and your real phone, WhatsApp, email, address, opening hours and social media links.

4. Using the admin — quick guide

Log in at yourdomain.com/wp-admin with the username and password you were given.

Good to know

  • If a contact field is left empty, the site shows the label only, with no link. Nothing is shown that you have not entered.
  • Product descriptions and page layouts are part of the custom design. For changes to these, ask the developer, who will update and re-upload the theme. This keeps the layout consistent.
  • The contact form needs a destination (an email service or form handler) to be connected before enquiries are received. This is set in the same Contact Details screen.
  • Take a backup before any update (Hostinger hPanel → Backups).
  • Use a strong password, and share admin access only with people you trust.

5. After launch

  • Ongoing care: update WordPress monthly and keep backups.
  • Search visibility: an SEO plugin (Yoast or Rank Math) and a Google Business Profile are recommended.
  • Changes: send the developer a list of text or image changes and they will be applied and uploaded together.
